# Sudan Displacement Intelligence

A four-page Power BI project examining internal displacement and return movements across Sudan using the public **IOM Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) Sudan Mobility Update 7**, dated **30 June 2026**.

## What the dashboard covers

- National overview of internally displaced people, IDP households, and returned individuals
- State-level comparison of displacement and return pressure
- Locality-level exploration and ranked caseload analysis
- Transparent methodology, metric definitions, sources, and limitations

## Featured analytical design

The State Pressure Matrix uses a diverging **pressure fingerprint**: IDP caseloads extend left and returned individuals extend right for each state. IDP-household totals are available in the tooltip, enabling direct comparison without the overlap of a bubble chart.

## Data snapshot

- 8,685,273 internally displaced people
- 1,742,414 IDP households
- 4,649,056 returned individuals
- 928,168 returnee households
- Coverage: 18 states and 185 localities

## Source and limitations

Source: IOM DTM Sudan, *IDPs and Returnees Snapshot 007*, 30 June 2026: https://dtm.iom.int/sudan

DTM figures are operational estimates and may be revised as verification and access conditions change. The combined populations-represented metric is the sum of reported IDPs and returned individuals; it is not a historical cumulative total or a count of unique people over time.
